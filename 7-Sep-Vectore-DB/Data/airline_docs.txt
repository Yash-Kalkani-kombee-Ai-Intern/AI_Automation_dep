DOCUMENT 1: Refund Policy

If a passenger cancels a flight, the eligible refund is processed within 7 working days. The refund is returned to the original payment method.


DOCUMENT 2: Baggage Policy

Passengers are allowed to carry up to 20 kg of checked baggage. Additional baggage may require extra charges.


DOCUMENT 3: Check-in Policy

Online check-in is available before departure. Passengers should complete check-in at least 45 minutes before the scheduled departure time.


DOCUMENT 4: Cancellation Policy

Cancellation charges depend on the type of ticket and how long before departure the cancellation is requested.


DOCUMENT 5: Flight Change Policy

Passengers can request a flight date or time change depending on ticket conditions. Change fees may apply.


DOCUMENT 6: Seat Selection Policy

Passengers can select their preferred seat during booking or check-in. Some seats may have an additional charge.
